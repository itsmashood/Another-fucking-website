I quit

The start of this year has honestly been one of the worst periods of my life. Everything has slowly started piling up and I feel like I have been fighting a losing battle against burnout, disappointment, and just general exhaustion. I used to enjoy creating things, scripting, and building projects, but lately it feels like every bit of motivation I had has been drained away.

Back in November 2025, my main Roblox account was terminated for something I genuinely did not do. Losing an account that I had spent so much time building was already frustrating, but having it happen over something that wasn't my fault made it even worse. Then in April 2026, I lost my new main account as well. It felt like another reminder that all the time and effort I put into things could disappear overnight.

On top of that, I didn't do as well as I wanted on my finals. It was another thing added to a list of failures and frustrations that kept growing. Eventually scripting stopped feeling like something I loved and started feeling like another responsibility hanging over me. Every project started feeling heavier, every line of code felt more tiring, and the passion I had slowly turned into stress.

So yeah, I think I'm done for now. I don't know if this is forever, but I know I need to step away. I am tired of forcing myself to create when I don't have anything left to give. Maybe one day I'll come back with a better mindset, but right now I just need to accept that I'm burnt out and move on.

I want to thank everyone who has been around during this time. Rarebae, Rana, Tariq, Zypher, Prime, Zara, and everyone else I've met through Discord — I genuinely appreciate all of you. The memories, conversations, and random moments mean more than you probably realize. I love you all and I will miss you all, but I think I need to distance myself from Discord too. It has become something that has taken more from me than it has given, and honestly it has been one of the biggest contributors to my already underdeveloped depression.

Thank you for everything. Goodbye, for now.
